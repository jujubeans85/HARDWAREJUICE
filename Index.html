PRESETS (this session)

Load a track or tap Use Mic, then Play. Drag a knob — or tap pad columns on a Launchpad Mini Mk2.
Honest Phase 0: real on-device DSP (EQ, drive, wobble-delay, air, wet/dry), not neural RAVE/AFTER — those come with the Mac. The Launchpad Mini Mk2 has no faders, so each of its 8 grid COLUMNS acts as a vertical light-fader: tap a pad high in a column for a high value, low for low. The pads light up to show the level. If pads light the wrong cells, the status bar prints the pad note when you press — adjust GRID_BASE / ROW_STEP near the top of this file.
